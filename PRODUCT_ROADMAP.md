# 🗺️ Product Roadmap

**Where we're going and why.**

---

## ✅ v1.0 - Foundation (November 2025)

**Status: LIVE** 🎉

### **What's Included:**
- ✅ Multi-account portfolio aggregation
- ✅ Sector and subsector allocation analysis
- ✅ Cash deployment intelligence with alerts
- ✅ Unknown stock detection and alerts
- ✅ Custom stock classification system
- ✅ 100% offline privacy
- ✅ Complete documentation (8 guides)

### **Why v1.0 Matters:**
Establishes core value: see ALL your accounts in one place with actionable insights your broker can't provide.

---

## 🔮 v2.0 - Performance Analytics (Coming Soon)

### **Planned Features:**

#### **📅 Time-Based Performance**
Track investment performance over time using purchase dates.

**You'll see:**
- Annualized returns per position
- Total return since purchase  
- Time-weighted portfolio performance
- Days/months/years held

**Why:** Understand which investments actually make money over time, not just current value.

---

#### **📊 Holding Period Analysis**
See how long you've held each position.

**You'll see:**
- Short-term vs long-term positions
- Tax lot identification
- Average holding period by sector
- Position aging analysis

**Why:** Make smarter tax decisions. Know which positions qualify for long-term capital gains.

---

### **Requirements for v2.0:**
- Purchase dates CSV (optional file)
- Format documented in SETUP_GUIDE
- Upload once, works forever

**Note:** Data model already supports this - just needs DAX measures and visuals.

---

## 🌟 v3.0 - Advanced Features (Under Consideration)

**Status: Exploring based on user feedback**

#### **💵 Dividend Income Tracking**
- Annual dividend income by stock & account
- Dividend yield analysis
- Income trend over time

AND / OR

### **Potential Features:**

#### **📡 Real-Time Data Integration**
Connect to market data APIs for live prices.

**Possibilities:**
- Auto-refresh prices during market hours
- Intraday performance tracking
- Price alerts

**APIs being considered:** Alpha Vantage (free), Yahoo Finance, IEX Cloud

---

#### **💰 Tax Planning**
Estimate tax liability before selling.

**Potential features:**
- Short-term vs long-term gains breakdown
- Tax-loss harvesting opportunities
- Cost basis visualization

---

#### **📊 Risk Analytics**
Understand portfolio risk profile.

**Potential metrics:**
- Beta (volatility vs market)
- Sharpe ratio
- Standard deviation
- Concentration risk

---

### **Why "Under Consideration"?**

We're launching v1.0 first to:
1. Get real user feedback
2. Understand actual needs (not assumed)
3. Prioritize features users actually want
4. Build based on demand, not guesses

**v3.0 will be shaped by YOU** - the users.

## 🎯 How Features Get Prioritized

**Decision factors:**
1. **User demand** - How many users request it?
2. **Impact** - How much value does it add?
3. **Feasibility** - How complex to build?
4. **Alignment** - Fits core mission (actionable insights)?

**Principles:**
- ✅ Free forever (no paywalls)
- ✅ Privacy first (local by default)
- ✅ User control (optional features)
- ✅ Professional quality
